# Characters

Use data-driven Rust configurations for acceleration, top speed, traction, jump impulse, airtime, spin, and ability parameters. Mario is baseline; Luigi gets jump/air control targets; Wario gets weight and charge; Waluigi gets agility/reach/aerial control. Engine-backed collision, animation, and interaction behavior is not implemented. All abilities must preserve mandatory campaign traversal, with a neutral fallback for progression-critical moves.

Wario and Waluigi model/animation sourcing needs a separate rights review. Only original or permissively licensed assets may be distributed; never extract or ship Nintendo assets.
